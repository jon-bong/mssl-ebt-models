    Properties

    ActionDate The date at which the transfer is to take effect. All transfers are deemed to take place at midnight(00:00:01) on the date of the transfer regardless of hh:mm:ss submitted.
    ActionDateString The string equivalent of ActionDate in yyyyMMddHHmmss format.
    BillingOption An identifier of the method under which the consumer will be billed. This will be either RetailerConsolidated i.e. RCB (Retailer Consolidated Billing) or Split i.e. SB (Split Billing).
    ConsumerName The name of the consumer account (100 characters max).
    EbsAccountNumber The EBS account number for the consumer (10 characters).
    EbtTransactionID An EBT Transaction ID consisting of the 10-digit Market Participant account number starting with 930 and a 6-character alphanumeric number left-padded with zeros, separated by a colon.
    (Inherited from EbtMessage)
    MeterOption An identifier of the Meter Option that the consumer has opted for.
    NewSupplierID A system participant who will be the new supplier of a consumer on and after the transfer date.
    Postcode The post code at which the premises is located (6 characters).
    SelfReadOption This field indicates whether customer has chosen Self Read Option (either X or blank).
    SendingPartyType For an EMC/DRA initiated request the value is SupplierElectronic indicating Supplier Electronic.
    UserID Identifies the user who sent the message.
